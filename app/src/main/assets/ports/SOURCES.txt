Sources of Netrik's port data

top-tcp.txt / top-udp.txt
  Numbers of the 1000 most common TCP and UDP ports, in the frequency order
  published by the Nmap project (https://nmap.org/). Only the port numbers are
  used; the nmap-services file, its frequencies and comments are not
  included.

services.tsv.gzip
  Service name per port and protocol (first registered name), from the IANA
  Service Name and Transport Protocol Port Number Registry
  (https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/).
